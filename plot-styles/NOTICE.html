Attribution and rights

Gunwoo Park authored the original paper plotting scripts, style and helper scripts. The current package uses one personal style with color/monochrome modes and no companion dependency.

Gunwoo Park directed the later reusable adaptation, integration, tests and documentation. Project-owned style, helpers, examples and reusable adaptations use the same MIT grant as wb-templates. Human direction and line-by-line review are separate.

Authentic batlow comes from the independently installed cmcrameri package: colormaps © 2020 Fabio Crameri; Python packaging © 2020 Callum Rollo; MIT. Its installed package’s license remains authoritative. Matplotlib, NumPy and other dependencies keep their own terms. No third-party library, font, original scientific data or publication PDF is bundled.

Style/paper and batlow references. The actual checked cmcrameri1.10 MIT notice is retained in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.

Matplotlib gallery reference and license are recorded in REFERENCES.md. The local preset stores selected gallery-style names and stroke/gap arrays along with researcher-specific lengths and aliases.
