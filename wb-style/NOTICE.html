Authorship and attribution

Original style and paper plotting scripts: Gunwoo Park. The Python scripts used to create the cited paper’s figures were researcher-authored, not generated by AI. This package derives presentation settings from those scripts; it does not claim that AI designed the original style or created the paper figures.

Source reference: G. W. Park, J. K. G. Dhont and G. Nägele, “Modeling crossflow filtration: Effect of shear on particle-enriched polarization and cake layers,” The Journal of Chemical Physics 164, 244119 (2026), doi:10.1063/5.0336005.

Gunwoo Park directed the later reusable adapter, neutral synthetic examples, tests, packaging and documentation.

See references for style and optional color-resource attribution, and LICENSE for the collection MIT grant covering project-owned style and adaptations. The examples use synthetic data.

Matplotlib gallery reference and license are recorded in REFERENCES.md. The local preset stores selected gallery-style names and stroke/gap arrays with researcher-specific dash lengths and aliases.
