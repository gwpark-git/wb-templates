License scope

The root MIT license is the shared grant for project-owned material identified below. Distinct copyright notices, scope statements and third-party terms remain with the material they cover. A detached export or wheel carries the license and notices applicable to its contents.

Material Grant and attribution Exceptions and standalone copies
Collection guides, templates and wb-python documentation Root MIT for authored material directed by Gunwoo Park wb-python is a documentation entry. Its independently distributed runtime has its own package grant.
wb-bd-mc Package MIT preserves the original 2020 and current attribution The combined package and its wheels carry this grant. Notices cover retained Apache-2.0 HI, PCG32 and other sources.
wb-mbla Root MIT for the new authored adaptation where the mBLA scope applies it Inherited C++ and fixtures have separate scope. Keep the third-party notices and level-specific scope files. CMake installation and detached export carry the shared MIT text as LICENSE.NEW.
mBLA profile explorer Authored viewer and retained renderer notices React terms and the original reference data are described in the profile explorer data guide. The data are outside the shared MIT grant.
scientific-io CSV MIT scope The wheel carries its two license files and notice.
plot-styles and wb-style Root MIT for project-owned presets, adapters and examples The wheel build reads local MIT copies and retains the plot-style notices and wb-style notices.
Quarto documentation example Root MIT for first-party source and synthetic data The independently copyable kit carries its copy and renderer and font notices.
BD–MC equilibrium EOS example Root MIT covers the new documentation The retained CSV and figures have separate research provenance.

GSL is separately installed GPL software linked by some C++ targets. This source candidate does not bundle linked binaries or establish combined binary release rights. NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, cycler and cmcrameri retain their actual terms. Authentic batlow data come from installed cmcrameri, not vendored data or an AI substitute. The documentation examples embed font subsets in rendered artifacts. The cited paper’s CC BY notice does not license separate source/data/model files.
