Matched conditions and estimators

The main groups used N=216N=216 in periodic cubic boxes with nominal volume fraction ϕ=πNσ3/(6V)\phi=\pi N\sigma^3/(6V). Here σ\sigma is the soft cutoff or hard diameter and ρ=N/V\rho=N/V is the particle number density. The CS reference is ZCS=(1+ϕ+ϕ2−ϕ3)/(1−ϕ)3Z_\mathrm{CS}=(1+\phi+\phi^2-\phi^3)/(1-\phi)^3 at the same nominal ϕ\phi. Carnahan and Starling (1969)

  • Soft BD. LAMMPS 22 Jul 2025 Update 4 used the cosine potential U(r)=A[1+cos(πr/σ)]U(r)=A[1+\cos(\pi r/\sigma)] inside the cutoff and zero beyond it. Pressure combines the ideal term and the pair virial. Three independent replicas per amplitude and concentration used timestep 10−5τσ10^{-5}\tau_\sigma, discarded 2τσ2\tau_\sigma and sampled 8τσ8\tau_\sigma at intervals of 0.002τσ0.002\tau_\sigma, where τσ=σ2/D0\tau_\sigma=\sigma^2/D_0 and D0D_0 is the bare diffusivity.
  • Hard-sphere MC. The C11 sampler accepts symmetric displacement trials only when they preserve excluded volume. Its sweep counts NN trials. Four independent replicas per concentration used proposal half-width 0.15σ0.15\sigma, discarded 10,000 sweeps and sampled 100,000 sweeps every five sweeps. The pressure estimator is Zx=1−ln(p)/xZ_x=1-\ln(p)/x with x=0.25x=0.25 and compression survival probability pp measured at V′=Vexp(−x/N)V'=V\exp(-x/N). This gives a finite-compression average of ZZ over a narrow density interval.

The error bars use the larger of the replica-based and pooled-block standard errors. For BD the block widths are 0.5, 1 and 2 Brownian times. For MC the block widths are 500, 1000 and 2000 sweeps for the later runs and 500, 1000 and 5000 for the archive. MC block errors propagate through the log-survival estimator. These descriptive bars quantify sampling variation. Equilibration, timestep, particle number and finite-compression bias are separate limitations.

Particle-number coverage

The 2026-11-25 comparison used only N=216N=216. The earlier study ran one N=512N=512 control per model at ϕ=0.30\phi=0.30. For soft BD at A/kBT=100A/k_\mathrm{B}T=100 with three replicas, ZZ changed from 3.315584 to 3.325197. For hard-sphere MC with four replicas and the same proposal width, ZZ changed from 3.976648 to 3.999224. These controls cover one state per model and sit outside the plotted main groups. Finite-size scaling and extrapolation to the thermodynamic limit remain unresolved. This example uses existing runs.
