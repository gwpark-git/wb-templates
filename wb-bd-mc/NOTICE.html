Attribution and license scope

The standard license texts and retained third-party notices are preserved.

Gunwoo Park is the original project author and directs this modernization. The original public project is https://github.com/gwpark-git/dynamics_of_networks_and_colloids.git. The upstream MIT copyright line Copyright (c) 2020 gunwoo is retained verbatim, with a separate 2026 attribution line in LICENSE. The standard MIT grant is unchanged. Copyrightability, third-party rights and recorded human review require their own evidence.

Included third-party material

Selected deterministic Stokesian-dynamics formulas and a resistance table derive from JFSD by William Torre and contributors, repository https://github.com/torrewk/Python-Jax-Fast-Stokesian-Dynamics, commit 5234c959c0fdb0ba7ba32d6015d0478014a512c7.

Retained terms and provenance:

  • Apache-2.0 license.
  • Original imported notice.
  • Source/formula provenance.
  • Frozen import hashes.

The derived third_party/hi_native/lib/hi_sd.cpp implementation and third_party/hi_native/third_party/hi_sd/resistance_table.h retain their Apache-2.0 provenance. See the HI notes for their scientific scope.

The optional standalone hard-sphere MC target compiles the retained unmodified PCG32 C source and header by Melissa O’Neill, from pcg-c-basic. The Apache-2.0 license and per-file copyright notices travel with those files. The EOS analysis reads LAMMPS output as data. LAMMPS retains its own license and notices.
