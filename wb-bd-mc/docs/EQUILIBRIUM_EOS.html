Periodic hard-sphere Monte Carlo and osmotic pressure

Scientific scope

The standalone sampler produces equilibrium configurations of monodisperse hard spheres in a periodic cubic box at fixed particle count and volume. The temperature enters the pressure normalization. The hard-core measure is uniform on nonoverlapping configurations. The sampler attaches no physical time to its updates. The soft reference experiment instead uses LAMMPS explicit overdamped Brownian dynamics with a finite cosine potential, Brownian time, and pair-force pressure. Both engines report EOS observables at the same nominal diameter, number density and temperature scale.

Physical and mathematical update

Use the particle diameter σ\sigma as the length unit. In a cube of side LL, the relative periodic displacement is the componentwise minimum image Δrij,α↦Δrij,α−Lround(Δrij,α/L)\Delta r_{ij,\alpha} \mapsto \Delta r_{ij,\alpha} - L\,\mathrm{round}(\Delta r_{ij,\alpha}/L). The set Ω\Omega contains configurations for which every minimum-image distance is at least one. The dimensionless potential is zero on Ω\Omega and +∞+\infty outside it.

One trial proceeds in this order.

  1. Select a particle label uniformly from 1,…,N1,\ldots,N, with replacement across trials. Draw each of three displacement components independently and uniformly in [−d,d][-d,d]. The native code uses the retained PCG32 generator with unbiased bounded label selection.
  2. Propose 𝐫i′=(𝐫i+𝛅)modL\mathbf r_i'=(\mathbf r_i+\boldsymbol{\delta})\bmod L. Inspect the 27 linked cells surrounding the target cell. Cell width is at least 1.05 diameters, so these cells cover all possible overlaps and the largest measured contact shell. The initialization also checks every pair independently of the cell list.
  3. Accept iff rij′2≥1r_{ij}'{}^2\geq 1 for every other particle. On acceptance update the wrapped coordinate, displacement bookkeeping and cell links. On rejection retain the previous particle state. The random stream has consumed the proposal in either outcome. The implementation rejects strictly below one in floating-point geometry.

The displacement law is symmetric on the torus, so the proposal density satisfies q(𝐑,𝐑′)=q(𝐑′,𝐑)q(\mathbf R,\mathbf R')=q(\mathbf R',\mathbf R). For two allowed configurations the Metropolis ratio is one, since both have the same Boltzmann weight. A proposed overlapping configuration has zero weight and is rejected, contributing a self-transition. This establishes detailed balance for the target configurational measure in the mathematical rule. Mixing and sampling error remain finite-run questions. One sweep counts NN such trials. The step dd controls acceptance and mixing. The optional unwrapped displacement is bookkeeping for the Markov chain. Physical Brownian diffusion, shear relaxation and viscosity require a dynamics model and a physical clock.

Soft Brownian model and physical time

The original LAMMPS study used the cosine interaction to vary repulsion at fixed nominal diameter and density with one parameter. The current comparison retains that law for direct replay. The outward radial force is (πA/σ)sin(πr/σ)(\pi A/\sigma)\sin(\pi r/\sigma) inside the cutoff. Both potential and force reach zero continuously there. The center barrier is 2A2A. Increasing A/kBTA/k_BT from 10 to 100 raises the energy cost of penetration while preserving the shape and nominal ϕ=πNσ3/(6V)\phi=\pi N\sigma^3/(6V). These amplitudes are finite. The diameter-based Brownian time is τσ=σ2/D0\tau_\sigma=\sigma^2/D_0, so a reduced time t*=nΔt*t^*=n\Delta t^* maps to t=t*τσt=t^*\tau_\sigma. At radius a=σ/2a=\sigma/2, the radius-based convention is τa=a2/D0=τσ/4\tau_a=a^2/D_0=\tau_\sigma/4. LAMMPS Brownian update, checked edition

In the hard MC run, one sweep counts NN trials of the configurational chain. Changing the trial width changes that chain's displacement per sweep while keeping its equilibrium target. A dynamics-based hard-sphere transport calculation would need its own evolution rule, collision treatment and time validation. Even the free-particle trial variance gives only a dilute reference for a dynamical mapping. The present MC trajectories establish equilibrium quantities such as pressure and contact structure.

Pressure observables and method references

With ρ=6ϕ/π\rho=6\phi/\pi in reduced units, the soft osmotic pressure is the ideal contribution ρ\rho plus the pair virial divided by 3V3V. The instantaneous virial is ∑i<j𝐫ij⋅𝐅ij\sum_{i<j}\mathbf r_{ij}\cdot\mathbf F_{ij}, with periodic minimum-image vectors and each unordered pair counted once. The LAMMPS observable uses compute pressure NULL pair. The ideal term uses kBTk_BT at the imposed bath temperature. The overdamped displacement increment serves the position update. For hard-sphere pressure, Allen and Tildesley give an explicit precedent in the companion NVT program for Computer Simulation of Liquids, 2nd ed. (Oxford University Press, 2017). In calc_variables the program shrinks the box length from LL to L/(1+ϵ)L/(1+\epsilon), counts the overlapping pairs CϵC_\epsilon, and uses Cϵ/(3ϵ)C_\epsilon/(3\epsilon) as the virial estimator. This estimates the small-compression limit using a pair count. Our study instead calculates the probability that the entire configuration survives the compression and uses a finite logarithmic difference. Transport observables and their interpretation

The equilibrium soft BD trajectories retain periodic image counters. Reconstruct 𝐫iunwrap(t)=𝐫i(t)+L𝐈i(t)\mathbf r_i^\mathrm{unwrap}(t)=\mathbf r_i(t)+L\mathbf I_i(t). Average |𝐫iunwrap(t+Δt)−𝐫iunwrap(t)|2\lvert\mathbf r_i^\mathrm{unwrap}(t+\Delta t)- \mathbf r_i^\mathrm{unwrap}(t)\rvert^2 over particles and available origins. The three-dimensional long-time Einstein relation uses one sixth of the MSD slope. A finite fitted window estimates tagged-particle Dseff/D0D_s^\mathrm{eff}/D_0. Compare slopes across windows before making an asymptotic claim. Long-wavelength collective diffusion is a separate property. See self and collective diffusion for the coherent density estimator, wavevector limits and the expected concentration trends.

For equilibrium constant-mobility BD without interparticle HI, an EOS-based thermodynamic estimate uses Dc/D0=(kBT)−1∂Π/∂ρ=∂ϕ[ϕZ(ϕ)]D_c/D_0=(k_BT)^{-1}\partial\Pi/\partial\rho=\partial_\phi[\phi Z(\phi)] at fixed potential and temperature. An interval secant estimates the average thermodynamic factor over that interval. A dilute endpoint may use limϕ→0Z=1\lim_{\phi\to0} Z=1. The estimate does not measure the time decay of collective density modes or a direction-dependent diffusivity under shear.

For viscosity use the symmetric configurational pair-pressure tensor in units kBT/σ3k_BT/\sigma^3, with only the pair forces of the soft model. With mean-subtracted deviatoric tensor δ𝚷d\delta\boldsymbol{\Pi}^d, the isotropic equilibrium Green–Kubo integrand is Gconf(t)=(V/kBT)⟨δ𝚷d(0):δ𝚷d(t)⟩/10G_\mathrm{conf}(t)=(V/k_BT)\langle \delta\boldsymbol{\Pi}^d(0):\delta\boldsymbol{\Pi}^d(t)\rangle/10. Integrate to a declared lag to obtain the finite configurational contribution. Its unit is kBTτσ/σ3k_BT\tau_\sigma/\sigma^3. Under the Stokes–Einstein diameter convention, adding the solvent once gives the partial ratio ηpartial/ηs=1+3πηconf*\eta_\mathrm{partial}/\eta_s=1+3\pi\eta_\mathrm{conf}^*. This includes the modeled configurational relaxation and the chosen solvent term. Hydrodynamic stresslets, lubrication and other missing stress terms require a separate model. The positional inputs are volume fraction, particles per side of a simple cubic start, displacement half-width, PCG seed and stream, discarded sweeps, production sweeps and sample stride. The optional final argument fcc starts from shuffled FCC sites with vacancies at the same particle count and volume. Its initialization RNG is separate from the MC proposal stream. The default is the earlier simple-cubic start. CSV records sweep, capped minimum separation squared, cumulative unordered counts for widths 0.0025 to 0.04, unwrapped MC displacement and acceptance. Clear physical interpretation and immutable scalar data make the observable usable by a separate process. The analysis example reads selected hard-MC CSV and LAMMPS run tables with explicit plans and archived comparators, and writes a common table and a PDF/PNG pair. Raw study data are separate from the template source.

The native tests check exact contact, periodic overlap, shell counting, ideal moves and equality of linked-cell and brute-force seeded trajectories. Level 3 exchange contract and acceptance scope

The reusable slice is equilibrium pressure and near-contact structure for monodisperse spheres in a quiescent periodic cube. The native producer owns its sampler state and PCG stream. An independent Python process can consume its CSV using NumPy and the functions in bdmc.equilibrium. The producer does not need Python, LAMMPS, mBLA or a wb plugin at runtime.

Boundary Contract
Hard-sphere observations Nine named columns in the order emitted by the CLI. sweep counts trial batches. min_r2_capped is the minimum squared separation in diameter units, capped at 1.0521.05^2. The five cumulative shell columns count unordered pairs. mc_msd is displacement in squared diameter units on the trial clock. acceptance is the fraction of accepted proposals in the latest output block.
Pressure exchange Z=Π/(ρkBT)Z=\Pi/(\rho k_BT), a dimensionless equilibrium osmotic pressure, paired with nominal ϕ\phi, NN, model identity and estimator. Hard MC uses a compression survival secant or a finite contact shell; LAMMPS soft BD uses the pair virial with the ideal term.
State and reproducibility Keep temperature/length conventions, potential and amplitude or hard exclusion, seed and stream, box and boundary conditions, equilibration, sampling cadence, fit/estimator windows, uncertainty and source/data edition alongside values.
Consumer and domain The comparison consumes pressure at sampled concentrations. Conversion to Pa needs the receiving model's kBTk_BT and number density. A table, interpolation and applicability checks belong to the receiving workflow. Dynamic coefficients or a new scale mapping require independent qualification.

The native test checks contact, periodic overlap, shell counts and seeded cell/brute-force agreement. The retained numerical replay checks the imported MC behavior against the original recorded observations. Independent physical evidence comes from the Carnahan–Starling comparison and a separate position-based check of the soft-BD virial. The comparable observable is pressure at a matched nominal state; the soft and hard interaction laws carry different model identities.
